# Voice Navigation User Guide

A simple guide for artisans to use voice navigation in Artisan Buddy.

## What is Voice Navigation?

Voice navigation lets you control the app using your voice instead of clicking buttons. Just speak commands like "go to dashboard" or "मुझे प्रोडक्ट क्रिएटर जाना है" and the app will take you there!

## How to Use

### Step 1: Find the Microphone Button

Look for the microphone icon (🎤) in the top-right corner of the screen, next to your profile picture.

### Step 2: Click to Activate

Click the microphone button. It will turn green when it's listening.

### Step 3: Speak Your Command

Speak clearly into your device's microphone. For example:
- "Go to product creator"
- "Show my inventory"
- "मुझे योजना दिखाओ"

### Step 4: Wait for Confirmation

The app will:
1. Show "Voice detected" when it hears you
2. Process your command
3. Navigate to the requested page
4. Give you audio feedback

## Tips for Best Results

### 1. Speak Clearly
- Don't speak too fast or too slow
- Pronounce words clearly
- Use a normal speaking voice

### 2. Quiet Environment
- Use in a quiet place
- Reduce background noise
- Move away from loud sounds

### 3. Good Microphone
- Hold device close to your mouth
- Make sure microphone is not blocked
- Check microphone is working

### 4. Natural Language
You can speak naturally! The system understands variations:
- "I want to go to product creator" ✅
- "Take me to the dashboard" ✅
- "Can you show me the schemes?" ✅
- "मुझे प्रोडक्ट बनाना है" ✅

### 5. Wait for Feedback
- Wait for the green indicator
- Listen for audio confirmation
- Don't speak again until processing is done

## Visual Indicators

### Microphone Button Colors

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Gray | Not listening (click to start) |
| Green | Listening (speak now) |
| Green + Pulse | Voice detected (processing) |
| Blue | Processing your command |
| Red | Error (try again) |

### Status Messages

- **"Listening..."** - Speak your command now
- **"Voice detected"** - System heard you
- **"Processing..."** - Understanding your command
- **"Navigating to..."** - Taking you to the page
- **"Error"** - Something went wrong, try again

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: "Microphone access denied"

**Solution**:
1. Click the lock icon in browser address bar
2. Allow microphone access
3. Refresh the page
4. Try again

### Problem: "No speech detected"

**Solution**:
- Speak louder
- Move closer to microphone
- Check microphone is working
- Try in a quieter place

### Problem: "Command not recognized"

**Solution**:
- Speak more clearly
- Use simpler words
- Try alternative commands
- Check language setting

### Problem: "Voice navigation not working"

**Solution**:
- Use Chrome, Edge, or Safari browser
- Update browser to latest version
- Check internet connection
- Refresh the page
